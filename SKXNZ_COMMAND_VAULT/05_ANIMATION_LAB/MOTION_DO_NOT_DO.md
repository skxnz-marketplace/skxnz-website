# MOTION — DO NOT DO

Banned motion patterns. Any of these = reject.

- ❌ Cartoon bounce / elastic overshoot.
- ❌ Slow, laggy transitions (anything that feels heavy).
- ❌ Random spinning / rotating elements.
- ❌ Too much neon / glow overload.
- ❌ Cheap hover effects (harsh color flips, jitters).
- ❌ Cluttered motion (too many things moving at once).
- ❌ Distracting background particles / floating junk.
- ❌ Distorted, stretched, or spun logo animation.
- ❌ Auto-playing loud/flashing effects.
- ❌ Motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Rule: if it doesn't read premium + purposeful, cut it.
