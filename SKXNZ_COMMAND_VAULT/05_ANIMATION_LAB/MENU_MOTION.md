# MENU MOTION (drawer / nav)

Priority #3.

- **Open:** slide in from side + fade, 240ms ease-out; backdrop dims to obsidian 40–60%.
- **Items:** stagger fade+rise, 30ms step.
- **Close:** reverse, 200ms.
- **Hover item:** chrome pulse / underline sweep, 120ms.
- **Header stays solid dark maroon** (no glass) even with drawer open.
- **Reduced motion:** instant open/close, no stagger.

Do NOT: bounce drawer, elastic overshoot, flashing items, heavy blur.
