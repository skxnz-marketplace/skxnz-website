# PRODUCT CARD MOTION

Priority #4. Cards must feel premium + dense (see [[UI_UX_MEMORY]]).

- **Hover:** lift 2–4px + soft shadow, 150ms ease-out; subtle glass-shine sweep on image.
- **Image:** gentle scale (1.02 max), no aggressive zoom.
- **Quick actions (wishlist/add):** fade in on hover, 120ms.
- **Entrance:** stagger fade+rise in grid, 40ms step.
- **Tap (mobile):** quick scale-down feedback (0.98), 100ms.
- **Reduced motion:** static; show actions always.

Do NOT: big bounce, flip, rotate, neon border pulse, laggy transitions.
