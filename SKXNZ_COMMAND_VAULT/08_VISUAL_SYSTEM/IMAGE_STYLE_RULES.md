# IMAGE STYLE RULES

## Look
- Editorial fashion photography, futurewear energy.
- Consistent color grade: deep shadows, clean highlights, hint of chrome/cyan/magenta.
- Product shots: clean, sharp, uniform aspect ratios for a seamless grid.

## Consistency
- Same crop ratios per grid section.
- Consistent background treatment (clean or obsidian).
- No mismatched lighting styles side by side.

## Integrity
- Original / licensed images only. No scraped competitor imagery.
- No fake model/endorsement claims.
- Optimize (WebP/AVIF) for performance.

## Avoid
Cheap stock look, inconsistent grading, distorted aspect ratios, watermarked images.
