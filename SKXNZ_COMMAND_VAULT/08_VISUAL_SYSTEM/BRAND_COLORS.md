## Brand accent palette (direction)
- Electric cyan · ultraviolet purple · sonic magenta · liquid silver · pearl white · chrome / glass-shine.
- Use accents sparingly — highlights, not fills. No neon overload.

## Usage
- Dark sections: obsidian / maroon-deep / wine.
- Header/nav: solid maroon.
- Content areas needing clarity: white / off-white.
- Accents: hover states, signals, small highlights only.
