# SKILL — SKXNZ Legal Clean Copy

**When:** writing any public-facing text (marketing, product, about, claims).
**Inputs:** draft copy + [[BRAND_MEMORY]].
**Process:**
1. Remove fake claims, fake metrics, fake partnerships.
2. Remove promises about unbuilt features (AI try-on, etc.) — only describe what exists.
3. Check no copying of competitor wording/slogans.
4. Keep premium tone; align to WEAR THE SIGNAL.
5. Flag anything needing legal review (guarantees, refunds, data claims).
**Output:** cleaned copy + flag list.
**Quality checklist:** true? original? no overpromise? premium tone? legally safe?
**Failure modes:** hype without proof, implying live features, copied slogans.
**SKXNZ rules:** truthful, original, buildable claims only.
