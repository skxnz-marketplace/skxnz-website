# Dead-code and honesty gate

The active route scan found many intentionally labelled private-preview/demo components. They were not broadly deleted: several remain imported by active routes, tests, compatibility shims, or preview workflows. Their presence is a launch risk, not a completed-product claim.

Confirmed active-copy changes in D8-A are limited to root metadata and local font/build behaviour. New regression checks protect against payment-live, refund-live, delivery-live, and protected-route sitemap leakage.

Remaining active preview/demo areas require product and operational review before public launch: waitlist/contact, seller analytics/inventory, community posting/moderation, legacy preview catalog fallbacks, and AI preview surfaces.
