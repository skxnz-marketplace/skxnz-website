# SKXNZ Legal and Safety Rules

## Purpose
This file keeps SKXNZ wording safe. It is not legal advice. Final policies should be reviewed by qualified legal and financial professionals before public launch.

## Before public operation
SKXNZ needs review for:

1. Terms of use.
2. Privacy policy.
3. Return and refund policy.
4. Shipping policy.
5. Seller terms.
6. Community guidelines.
7. AI disclaimer.
8. Support process.
9. GST/tax requirements where applicable.
10. Payment gateway compliance.

## Payment wording
Until payments are live, use:

```text
Demo checkout for internal testing.
```

Do not say live secure payments are enabled.

## Delivery wording
Until delivery operations are live, use:

```text
Delivery tracking is not live in MVP.
```

Do not claim guaranteed same-day delivery.

## AI wording
Until real AI features are connected and tested, use:

```text
AI features are in MVP/demo mode.
```

For future try-on:

```text
AI previews are generated for visual guidance only. Actual fit, color, fabric fall, and appearance may vary.
```

## Seller wording
Until seller verification is live, use:

```text
Seller onboarding is currently in review/beta.
```

Do not say all sellers are fully verified.

## Community safety
Before public posting, SKXNZ must have report, hide, block, moderation queue, takedown, and admin review systems.
