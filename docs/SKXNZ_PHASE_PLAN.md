# SKXNZ Phase Plan

## Phase 0 - Planning, safety, and repo control
Goal: add docs, safety rules, repo audit, QA checklist, and handoff plan. No feature merge.

## Phase 1 - Design system and homepage foundation
Goal: continue the existing homepage/theme work safely. Preserve the current dark maroon glass header, white premium background direction, compact marketplace structure, product grid, and working floating AI assistant.

## Phase 2 - Search, filters, and product discovery
Goal: make product discovery reliable using structured data for products, brands, categories, collections, tags, and search keywords.

## Phase 3 - Product detail and buyer conversion
Goal: improve product detail, image gallery, size/color selection, cart integration, wishlist integration, and related products.

## Phase 4 - Cart and demo checkout
Goal: make the buyer flow internally testable without live payments.

## Phase 5 - Buyer account and saved style
Goal: improve account, wishlist, saved products, order history placeholders, and preference capture.

## Phase 6 - Signal Community MVP
Goal: plan and then build `/community` only after moderation and reporting rules are ready.

## Phase 7 - Seller onboarding and seller dashboard
Goal: improve seller application, product upload, product review state, order placeholders, and seller status clarity.

## Phase 8 - Admin operations shell
Goal: improve admin product approval, seller approval, order management, return/refund placeholders, support queue, and later community moderation.

## Phase 9 - AI beta systems
Goal: evolve the current AI Phase 1 foundation and floating assistant without claiming unsupported live AI features.

## Phase 10 - Mobile polish
Goal: verify 320px, 375px, 430px, 768px, 1024px, and desktop breakpoints.

## Phase 11 - Private beta QA
Goal: test routes, forms, visual quality, placeholder labels, image fallbacks, accessibility, and no-fake-claims rules.

## Phase 12 - Public launch preparation
Goal: only after legal pages, support, payments, seller review, analytics, hosting, and monitoring are ready.
