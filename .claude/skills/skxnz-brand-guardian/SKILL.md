# SKXNZ Brand Guardian

## When to use
Any copy/naming/visual decision that could go off-brand, resemble another brand, or overclaim.

## Inputs needed
- The proposed text/design.
- `SKXNZ_COMMAND_VAULT/02_PROJECT_MEMORY/BRAND_MEMORY.md`, `08_VISUAL_SYSTEM/*`.

## Process
1. Check identity: premium futurewear, tagline WEAR THE SIGNAL, Gen Z, AI-powered.
2. Check palette + cues (obsidian/maroon/cyan/ultraviolet/magenta/silver, glass-shine, chrome).
3. Scan for resemblance to real brands (name, slogan, layout, logo). Reject copies.
4. Remove fake claims / unbuilt-feature promises.
5. Confirm logo integrity (never distorted).

## Output format
`PASS` or a `FIX` list: `issue → fix` (one line each).

## Quality checklist
On-brand? Original? Truthful? Logo safe? Premium?

## Failure modes to avoid
Copying competitors; childish/cheap look; inventing features; distorting logo; hype without proof.

## SKXNZ-specific rules
Never copy brands. Never fake. Premium + buildable only. Logo sacred.
