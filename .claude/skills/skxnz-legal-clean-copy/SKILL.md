# SKXNZ Legal Clean Copy

## When to use
Any public copy: marketing, product, about, FAQ, claims, guarantees.

## Inputs needed
- Draft copy.
- `SKXNZ_COMMAND_VAULT/02_PROJECT_MEMORY/BRAND_MEMORY.md`, `PRODUCT_MEMORY.md` (what actually exists).

## Process
1. Remove fake claims/metrics/partnerships.
2. Remove promises about unbuilt features (e.g. AI try-on) — describe only what exists.
3. Check no copied competitor wording/slogans.
4. Keep premium tone aligned to WEAR THE SIGNAL.
5. Flag guarantees/refunds/data/privacy claims for legal review.

## Output format
Cleaned copy + flag list (items needing legal review).

## Quality checklist
True? Original? No overpromise? Premium tone? Legally safe?

## Failure modes to avoid
Hype without proof, implying live-but-unbuilt features, copied slogans, unverifiable stats.

## SKXNZ-specific rules
Truthful, original, buildable claims only. Match verified build state in `PRODUCT_MEMORY`.
